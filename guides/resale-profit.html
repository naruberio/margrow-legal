How to calculate resale profit after fees and shipping

Your profit is what the buyer paid, minus fees and shipping, minus what you paid for the item. Keep it per item, and the same formula answers three questions: what to list at, whether to take an offer, and what you actually made.

The formula

Take-home = sale price + shipping paid by buyer − fees − shipping label cost − other costs
Profit = take-home − cost
Margin = profit ÷ sale price

Steps in Margrow

  1. On the Inventory tab, tap + at the bottom right and choose Add one item. Enter the Name, Cost and Bought on date, then tap Save. If there is an amount you won’t sell below, add it as the Lowest price.
  2. On the item, open Set List Price, set Price by to Profit or Margin and enter your target. List price by marketplace shows a list price per marketplace with fees included, with the take-home and profit for each. Pick one with List at this price.
  3. When a buyer makes an offer, open Check an Offer and enter the Offer amount. You get OK to accept, Below your lowest price, You’d lose money or Under your target margin.
  4. When it sells, open Mark as Sold and enter Sold for, Shipping paid by buyer, Shipping label cost and Other costs. Fees is filled in from the calculation, and your Profit is shown. Tap Record sale.

To check before you buy, use Max you should pay on the Sourcing tab: enter the Expected sale price, where you’ll Sell on, the Shipping cost and a target margin or ROI, and Pay up to shows the most you can pay.

Questions

How do you calculate resale profit?
Take-home = sale price + shipping paid by the buyer − fees − shipping label cost − other costs. Profit = take-home − what you paid for the item. Margrow works out each item’s profit this way.
What is margin measured against?
In Margrow, margin is profit as a share of the sale price.
Where do marketplace fees come from?
Fees for marketplaces such as eBay, Poshmark, Mercari and Etsy come built in, each with the date they were checked. If a marketplace changes its fees, edit them in Settings under Marketplace fees.
How do I know whether to accept an offer?
Enter the offer in Check an Offer. Margrow works out your take-home and profit after fees and shipping, and checks the offer against your lowest price and target margin. If it falls short, it suggests a counter amount.
Where are my records stored?
Everything stays on your iPhone. There is no account to sign up for.
