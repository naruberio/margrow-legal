# Privacy Policy

Margrow for iPhone · Effective October 27, 2026 · [日本語](./privacy-policy-ja)

Margrow (formerly FlipProfit) is provided by Naruberio LLC (合同会社ナルベリオ, "we"). This page explains what happens to the information you put into the app.

## Summary

- Your ledger lives in a database on your iPhone. We never receive it.
- The app has no account, no server, no analytics, no ads, and no tracking.
- The app does not connect to the internet.
- A file leaves your iPhone only when you export it and choose where to send it.

## What you enter, and where it stays

You record items, purchase and sale prices, marketplaces, fees, expenses, mileage, notes, and photos you attach. If you turn on the secondhand-dealer register (古物台帳, for Japan), you may also record the name, address, and ID details of people you bought from.

All of this is stored in a database inside the app's own storage on your iPhone. iOS protects it with the device's file encryption while the phone is locked. If iCloud Backup or computer backups are on, the data is included in those backups, which Apple or your computer hold — not us.

**We cannot see, recover, or delete this data for you.** Deleting the app deletes it. Keep a backup with the app's Export & Backup screen if your records matter to you.

### Records about other people

The details you record about sellers or buyers are yours to manage. If the law where you trade requires you to keep, protect, or erase them (for example, Japan's Secondhand Articles Dealer Act), that duty is yours as the dealer. Only record what you need, and be careful where you send exported files.

## Data we collect

None. We declare "Data Not Collected" in the App Store privacy details.

## Permissions

| Permission | Why | If you say no |
|---|---|---|
| Notifications | Reminders about items that have not sold after the number of days you set | No reminders. Everything else works |
| Camera (only if you take a photo in the app) | One photo per item | Pick a photo from your library instead |

Photos you pick from your library come through the iOS photo picker, which hands the app only the photo you choose. The app does not ask for location, contacts, or tracking permission.

## Files you export or import

Backups and CSV files are created only when you ask. iOS then shows its share sheet, and the file goes wherever you choose (Files, Mail, AirDrop, and so on). Exported files can contain the personal details described above, so the app warns you before it writes them. CSV files you import from marketplaces are read on your iPhone and are not sent anywhere.

## Third parties

The app contains no third-party SDKs. Apple processes your purchase of the app; see [Apple's Privacy Policy](https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/).

Earlier FlipProfit versions used RevenueCat to check an in-app purchase and fetched exchange rates online. Margrow removes both.

## Children

Margrow is a business tool and is not directed at children.
